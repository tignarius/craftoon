Turn your ideas into comics, one panel at a time.

Craftoon is a drawing space for making comics and storyboards on iPad. Arrange your panels, sketch your scenes, and preview the pages as a story.

BUILD YOUR PAGE
Start with a portrait or landscape canvas. Drag on the blank page to create panels, or use a grid template. Move and resize panels in Layout mode, with alignment guides to help keep your page tidy.

DRAW INSIDE EACH PANEL
Switch a panel to Draw mode and use the familiar PencilKit tools with Apple Pencil. Sketch, erase, choose colors, and select strokes with the built-in lasso. Drawing stays inside the panel, so you can focus on the scene.

TRACE YOUR OWN REFERENCES
Import an image from Photos or Files into an individual panel. Move, rotate, and scale the reference, then adjust its opacity for tracing. Image controls stay in the panel, and changing the reference does not move the panel. Hide the reference to check your drawing, or collapse its controls to continue sketching.

READ AND ORGANIZE
Preview your comic in Read mode. In Library, tap a cover to show Edit, Read, and Delete actions at its center. Tap an empty area to clear the selection, or use Undo after deleting a comic.

START FREE
Create and draw comics with up to four pages per comic, and save one comic in Library. Edit your saved comic, read your work and try panel layouts and tracing tools.

UNLOCK CRAFTOON PRO
A one-time in-app purchase unlocks saving more comics to Library, adding more than four pages, emailing comics as PDF attachments, and printing. Restore your Pro purchase from the app menu.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Tap the mode icon at the top left for the one-page Quick Guide, app information, purchase restoration, and activation code entry.
