Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains how SquarePlayer handles information in Craftoon for iPad and on its support website.

Craftoon does not require an account. Your drawings and comic files are processed on your iPad. The app does not automatically upload your artwork to SquarePlayer, and it does not include advertising or third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Your drawings and pictures

Comic pages, drawings, titles, and imported reference images are stored in the app's local storage. Photo access is used to import pictures you choose. SquarePlayer does not receive your photo library or your comics through normal drawing and saving.

Your device's backup settings may include app data in backups managed by Apple or by you. Craftoon does not provide a separate SquarePlayer cloud-sync service.

Purchases

Apple processes Craftoon Pro purchases and purchase restoration through the App Store. Craftoon uses StoreKit to check your Pro access on the device. The app does not send individual transaction records to a SquarePlayer server, and SquarePlayer does not receive your payment card details through the app.

Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and printing

If you choose to share a PDF, Craftoon passes the selected comic to Apple's Mail composer or the system sharing interface. You choose the recipient or destination. Printing passes your comic to the printer you select through the system printing interface.

These actions can send your content outside your iPad. The chosen email provider, sharing service, or printer handles that content under its own terms. Craftoon does not automatically send copies to SquarePlayer.

Support emails

If you email craftoon.support@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments or technical details you choose to include so that we can respond to your request. This information is processed through Gmail. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or private artwork that is not needed to explain the problem.

You can contact us to ask about, or request deletion of, information you sent in a support email. Any information that must be retained to resolve a dispute or meet legal obligations may need to be kept. Gmail's processing is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

This support website

This website does not include analytics scripts, advertising, or a contact form. GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as described in its Privacy Statement. This website hosting activity is separate from the app's on-device drawing features.

Young creators

The app does not ask children to create accounts or submit personal information. Young creators should ask a parent or guardian to contact support. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address, contact us to request its deletion.

Your choices

  • Manage photo access in your iPad's Settings.
  • Delete saved comics from Library. Removing the app deletes its local data from that device; backup copies and exported files are managed separately.
  • Choose whether and where to share or print your comics.
  • Contact us about personal information included in a support request.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the practices described here change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
