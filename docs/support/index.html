A FEW LITTLE ANSWERS

Wondering about something?

That's what we're here for.

Where is my comic?

Open Library and tap your comic's cover. Choose Canvas to keep creating, or Read to enjoy your story.

Want a new name? Tap the comic's title in Library.

Can I change the boxes?

Yes! Use Layout mode to move and resize panels. You can make them tall, wide, or give them slanted sides.

Can I draw over a picture?

Yes. Add your own picture from Photos or Files to a panel. Make it lighter with the opacity control, then draw over it. Hide the picture when you want to see just your drawing.

What can I do for free?

You can draw, change panels, use picture references, and read your comics. Each comic can have up to four pages. You can save one comic in Library and keep working on it.

What is Craftoon Pro?

Pro gives you more room: save more comics, add more than four pages, send PDFs, and print your stories.

Pro is an optional one-time purchase, not a subscription. You can see the price in the app. If you're a child, ask a parent or guardian before buying.

We already bought Pro. How do we get it back?

Use the same Apple Account that bought Pro. Connect to the internet, open the Craftoon app menu, and tap Restore Purchases.

Still stuck? A parent or guardian can email us for help.

How do I send or print my comic?

These features need Pro. Open Read mode, then tap the envelope to send a PDF, or the printer to print.

Email needs Mail set up on your iPad. Printing needs an AirPrint printer on the same Wi-Fi network. Ask a grown-up if you need help setting these up.

Where is the guide inside the app?

Tap the current mode icon in the top-left corner. Open Quick Guide for a little reminder of how things work.
